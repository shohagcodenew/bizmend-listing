OpenAI at a glance

A concise profile summary of OpenAI’s company focus, product areas, and role in the AI software market.

OpenAI is an AI research and deployment company that develops advanced AI models, consumer AI products, developer tools, and business AI solutions.

The company is best known for ChatGPT, GPT models, the OpenAI API, Codex, and AI tools used across writing, coding, research, customer support, education, content generation, and enterprise workflows.

“Our mission is to ensure that artificial general intelligence benefits all of humanity.”

User Groups

Market Category

COMPANY TIMELINE

Company Timeline / Key Milestones

Key milestones that shaped OpenAI, from founding to major product, funding, partnership and expansion events.

2015Founded· Dec 11, 2015

OpenAI Founded

OpenAI was founded as a non-profit research lab with the mission to ensure that artificial general intelligence (AGI) benefits all of humanity. The company was established by a group of entrepreneurs and researchers who recognized the importance of developing AI safely and responsibly.

2019Product Release· Feb 14, 2019

GPT-2 Model Released

OpenAI released GPT-2, a powerful unsupervised language model with 1.5 billion parameters. The release demonstrated major progress in natural-language generation and helped increase attention around large-scale language models.

2021Strategic Partnership· Jul 22, 2021

Partnership with Microsoft

OpenAI announced a multi-year strategic partnership with Microsoft to support advanced AI research, cloud infrastructure, and development of next-generation AI technologies.

2022Product Launch· Nov 30, 2022

ChatGPT Public Launch

OpenAI launched ChatGPT to the public as a conversational AI assistant. The product quickly gained large-scale adoption and became an important milestone in bringing generative AI into everyday consumer and business use.

2023Funding Round· Jan 23, 2023

Major Investment Round

OpenAI announced a significant investment round to support AI research, product development, infrastructure growth, and wider deployment of its AI systems and services.

2024Global Expansion· May 13, 2024

Expanded Global Presence

OpenAI expanded its international presence with additional offices and teams across major regions, supporting its growing products, partnerships, research activities, and global operations.
