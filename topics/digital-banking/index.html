TOPIC OVERVIEW

Definition and overview

Digital banking means using online platforms, mobile apps and connected financial tools to manage accounts, payments, cards and transfers.

These services help people and businesses manage money, control spending, send payments and access banking tools without branch-first workflows.

What it means

Banking services delivered through apps, platforms and connected financial infrastructure.

What it covers

Online accounts, mobile banking, cards, payments and banking APIs.

Why it matters

Digital banking makes finance more accessible, connected and easier to manage.

EVOLUTION
